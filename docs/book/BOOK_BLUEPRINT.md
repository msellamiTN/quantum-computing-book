# Book Blueprint — 24 Chapters

Six learning paths with four chapters each. The sequence is progressive: applications follow the foundations needed to reason about them.

| Learning path | Chapters | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Getting Started | 1–4 | Information, mathematics, states, tensor products |
| Quantum Circuits | 5–8 | Bloch sphere, gates, entanglement, circuit design |
| Qiskit Engineering | 9–12 | Environment, construction, simulation, testing |
| Quantum Algorithms | 13–16 | Algorithmic reasoning and foundational algorithms |
| Applications | 17–20 | Communication, chemistry, optimization, QML |
| Responsible Engineering | 21–24 | Noise, error correction, hardware, end-to-end project |

## Part I — Foundations
1. What Is Quantum Computing? / Qu’est-ce que l’informatique quantique ?
2. The Mathematics You Need / Les mathématiques indispensables
3. Quantum States and Dirac Notation / États quantiques et notation de Dirac
4. Multiple Qubits and Tensor Products / Plusieurs qubits et produits tensoriels

## Part II — Quantum Circuits
5. Qubits, Measurement and the Bloch Sphere / Qubits, mesure et sphère de Bloch
6. Quantum Gates / Portes quantiques
7. Superposition, Interference and Entanglement / Superposition, interférence et intrication
8. Design Your First Quantum Circuits / Concevoir ses premiers circuits quantiques

## Part III — Qiskit Engineering
9. Set Up Your Quantum Development Environment / Préparer son environnement quantique
10. Build and Visualize Circuits / Construire et visualiser des circuits

12. Test and Debug Quantum Programs / Tester et déboguer des programmes quantiques

## Part IV — Quantum Algorithms

## Part VI — Responsible Engineering
21. Noise and Error Mitigation / Bruit et atténuation des erreurs
22. Introduction to Quantum Error Correction / Introduction à la correction d’erreurs quantiques
23. Quantum Hardware and Benchmarking / Matériel quantique et benchmarking
24. Build an End-to-End Quantum Project / Construire un projet quantique de bout en bout

## Progressive capstone
A manufacturing scheduling case study is staged: (1) formulate and solve classically; (2) isolate a small explicit subproblem; (3) construct and validate a tiny QUBO; (4) experiment with QAOA; (5) compare with exact enumeration and a classical baseline. The reduced subproblem must never be represented as solving the complete industrial schedule.

## Status
Chapter 1 is the first prototype. Chapters 2–4 need complete manuscripts, original figures and executable companion labs before the foundations block is publication-ready.
